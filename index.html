B-Day Connect Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to B-Day Connect. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and protect it, and the choices available to you when using the application.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect your email address, username, date of birth, gender, country, city, profile information, profile photos, gallery photos, posts, comments, reactions, private messages, notification preferences, and device notification tokens.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to create and manage your account, help users find birthday twins, display profiles, provide messaging and social features, deliver notifications, improve the Service, prevent abuse, maintain security, and respond to support requests.

4. Information Visible to Other Users

Your username, profile information, birthday details, photos, posts, comments, reactions, and other activity may be visible to other users depending on your privacy settings and how you use the Service.

5. User Content

Photos, posts, comments, messages, reactions, and other content you submit may be stored on B-Day Connect servers. You are responsible for ensuring that you have the right to upload and share this content.

6. Authentication and Account Security

Passwords are not stored in plain text. Authentication tokens may be stored locally on your device to keep you signed in. You are responsible for protecting access to your device and account.

7. Push Notifications

B-Day Connect uses Firebase Cloud Messaging to send notifications about messages, friend requests, likes, comments, birthdays, and other account activity. You may manage notifications through the application or your device settings.

8. Third-Party Services

B-Day Connect may use trusted third-party services such as Firebase Cloud Messaging, Google Play Services, Apple Push Notification Service, hosting providers, and other technical infrastructure required to operate the Service.

9. Data Storage and Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

10. Data Retention

We retain personal information while your account is active and for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Service, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

11. Account Deletion

You may request account deletion through the application. After deletion, your profile and associated information may be permanently removed, except where limited retention is required for legal, technical, or security purposes.

12. Children's Privacy

B-Day Connect is intended only for users who meet the minimum age required under applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals who are not legally permitted to use the Service.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version and revision date will be published within the application and/or on this page.

14. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal information, contact us at bdayconnect@gmail.com
