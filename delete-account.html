B-Day Connect – Account Deletion

B-Day Connect users can request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

Delete your account in the app

You can delete your account directly from the B-Day Connect app using the account deletion option in your account settings.

Request account deletion

If you cannot access the app, you can request account deletion by contacting us at:

Email: bdayconnect@gmail.com

Please send the request from the email address associated with your B-Day Connect account and state that you want your account deleted.

Data that will be deleted

When your account is deleted, the account and personal data associated with it will be deleted, including profile information and user-generated content associated with the account, subject to any data that must be retained where required by law.
