B-Day Connect Child Safety Standards

Last updated: September 27, 2026

B-Day Connect is committed to protecting children and maintaining a safe environment for all users. B-Day Connect has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

1. Prohibited Content and Behavior

B-Day Connect strictly prohibits:

  • Child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE).
  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM).
  • Grooming or attempting to sexually exploit a minor.
  • Sexualization of minors.
  • Sextortion or sexual coercion involving minors.
  • Trafficking or exploitation of children for sexual purposes.
  • Soliciting, creating, uploading, sharing, distributing, or requesting sexual content involving minors.
  • Any other content or behavior that sexually exploits, abuses, or endangers a child.

These standards apply to profiles, posts, comments, messages, images, videos, and other user-generated content or communications available through B-Day Connect.

2. Reporting Safety Concerns

B-Day Connect provides an in-app reporting mechanism for users to report safety concerns.

To report a specific account, users can open the user's profile and select Report user from the profile menu.

Users can also submit a general safety report from:

Settings → Safety → Report a safety concern

Reports may include child safety concerns, sexual exploitation, harassment, abuse, fake profiles, spam, or other unsafe behavior.

3. Review and Enforcement

Safety reports submitted through B-Day Connect are reviewed and may result in appropriate enforcement action.

Depending on the nature of the violation, B-Day Connect may remove prohibited content, restrict access to features, suspend accounts, permanently terminate accounts, or take other appropriate measures.

When B-Day Connect becomes aware of prohibited child sexual abuse material or child sexual exploitation, appropriate action will be taken in accordance with applicable child safety laws and reporting obligations.

4. Cooperation with Authorities

B-Day Connect will cooperate with lawful requests from law enforcement agencies and appropriate child-safety authorities as required by applicable law.

Where required by applicable law, suspected CSAM or child sexual exploitation may be reported to the appropriate authorities or child protection organizations.

If a child or any other person is in immediate danger, users should contact their local emergency services or law enforcement agency.

5. Scope

These Child Safety Standards apply to the B-Day Connect application and user-generated content and communications made available through B-Day Connect.
