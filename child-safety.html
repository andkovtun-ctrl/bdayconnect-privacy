B-Day Connect Child Safety Standards

B-Day Connect is committed to providing a safe environment for all users and has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE).

Prohibited Content and Behavior

B-Day Connect strictly prohibits any content or behavior involving child sexual abuse, child sexual exploitation, grooming, sexualization of minors, trafficking of minors, or any other form of sexual exploitation involving children.

Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM), including images, videos, messages, links, or any other content that depicts or promotes the sexual abuse or exploitation of children, is strictly prohibited.

Reporting Child Safety Concerns

Users who encounter content or behavior that may involve child sexual abuse or exploitation should report it immediately.

Child safety concerns can be reported to: bdayconnect@gmail.com

Enforcement

B-Day Connect may remove prohibited content, restrict or permanently suspend accounts involved in violations, and take other appropriate enforcement actions.

Where required by applicable law, reports involving suspected child sexual abuse material or child exploitation may be reported to the appropriate authorities or child protection organizations.

Cooperation with Authorities

B-Day Connect will cooperate with lawful requests from law enforcement agencies and other appropriate authorities concerning child safety, child sexual abuse, and child exploitation.

Contact

For questions regarding these Child Safety Standards, contact: bdayconnect@gmail.com
